Backmark

Point your camera at an old object and learn what it is, what it is probably worth, and how sure the app is.

What it does

  • Reads the maker's mark when the photo shows one, and says Strong match only when that mark spells the maker.
  • Shows up to three possibilities with a confidence word and the reason behind it, instead of one confident answer.
  • Says may be valuable, get it appraised when an item could be worth a lot and the identification is uncertain, instead of printing a price.
  • Keeps your collection, photos and notes on your iPhone. No account, no server of ours.

What it is not

Values are AI estimates, not a professional appraisal. They vary with condition and market. For anything you plan to sell or insure, see a qualified appraiser.
