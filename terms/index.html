What the app does, and what it does not claim

Backmark identifies objects from photos and estimates what they may be worth. The estimates are produced by an AI model from a photo. They are not a professional appraisal, they are not an offer to buy or sell, and they can be wrong. Condition, provenance, repairs and the market all change what an item is worth.

Before you sell, insure, buy or part with anything on the strength of a result, have the item seen by a qualified appraiser. You are responsible for decisions you make about your items.

Purchases

Backmark gives you three scans free. After that, identification needs a plan. There are three options, and the app shows the full price of each above the purchase button before you buy:

  • A weekly subscription, renewing every week until cancelled.
  • A yearly subscription, renewing every year until cancelled.
  • A one-time lifetime purchase, which does not renew.

There is no free trial, so nothing starts charging quietly. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings on your iPhone; the app also links to that screen in Settings. Purchases can be restored on the paywall and in Settings.

Payment is taken by Apple. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by the developer.

Fair use

A plan is for one person's own items. Automated or bulk use, resale of the app's answers as a service, and attempts to work around the app's limits are not permitted.

Availability

Identification needs a connection and a third-party AI service. That service can be slow or unavailable. Your collection, saved results, folders and notes keep working without a connection, because they are stored on your iPhone.
